# Stark Overflow judge demonstration

The audience is datathon judges inspecting a reproducible discount-policy investigation system. Success is understanding the weekly model, changing employee/assumption controls, and reproducing the numerical demo without private data or a Databricks account.

The public demo is a synthetic adaptation of the existing weekly-model dashboard. Preserve its population, employee history and assumption-sensitivity tasks. All people, restaurants and observations in the demo are invented; no policy-violation outcomes or measured fraud effectiveness are supplied. Source data and review decisions stay in authorized Databricks workspaces.

The team repository already owns its semantic SQL and Genie configuration; preserve those contributions. The standalone demo uses standard browser controls and responsive statistical plots. It inherits the existing dashboard's restrained, readable chart layout; this task does not create a new brand or redesign the private application.
